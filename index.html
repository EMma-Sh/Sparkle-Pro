Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Are your cleaners background-checked? +
Yes! All our cleaners pass comprehensive background checks and are fully insured. Your safety and security are our top priority.
What cleaning products do you use? +
We use eco-friendly, non-toxic cleaning products that are safe for children, pets, and the environment. We can also use your preferred products if you like.
How much does cleaning cost? +
Pricing depends on your home size and service type. Use our instant calculator on the homepage to get an accurate quote. Standard clean starts at $100.
Can I schedule last-minute cleanings? +
Absolutely! If we have availability, we can schedule a cleaning within 24 hours. Use our app or call us at (555) 012-3456 to check availability.
What if I'm not satisfied with the cleaning? +
We guarantee 100% satisfaction. If you're not happy with any aspect of our service, we'll re-clean those areas at no charge.
Do you offer recurring cleaning schedules? +
Yes! We offer weekly, bi-weekly, and monthly scheduling options. Recurring customers enjoy a 10% discount on all services.
